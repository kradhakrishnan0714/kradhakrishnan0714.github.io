**The problem**: Delivery teams routinely stalled waiting for realistic test data. Production data was off-limits, and building multi-table datasets by hand that respected every schema relationship and business rule took days.

**What I built**: I conceived and launched an agentic AI platform of custom agents and reusable skills:

- **Generation agents** interpret data catalogs, database schemas, table relationships and business rules from a plain-language request, then produce enterprise-scale, multi-table datasets
- **Validation agents** check schema compliance, referential integrity, business constraints and data quality before any dataset is released, creating a self-checking loop
- **Reusable skills** for metadata discovery, relationship inference and multi-table generation, in a modular architecture that scales across applications and data domains

**Tech Stack**: Python, SQL, custom agent framework with reusable skills

**Impact**: Cut test data preparation time by 60% and eliminated more than 90% of data availability blockers.
