Hello! I'm **Karthik**, a product-minded quality leader who builds the platforms that tell organizations whether their **Generative AI, LLM, RAG and agentic systems** are ready to ship.

At **PwC** in Dallas I own the vision and roadmap for reusable GenAI evaluation capabilities: automated assessment of faithfulness, hallucination, retrieval accuracy and instruction adherence, wired into CI/CD as release gates that engineering and business teams rely on. I also conceived and launched an **agentic AI platform** whose agents generate enterprise test data and other agents check it, cutting data preparation time by 60% and removing over 90% of data availability blockers.

Before that I spent 14 years at Hexaware, much of it on financial services data, including validating **credit risk models** and leading QA teams of 5 to 12 on large **auto finance** platforms. That background shapes how I think about AI: quality is a product decision, and it has to hold up in regulated environments.

I recently completed an **M.S. in Data Science** at **Southern Methodist University (2024–2026)**. I enjoy building strong teams, aligning stakeholders around clear quality bars, and turning evaluation results into decisions leaders can act on.
